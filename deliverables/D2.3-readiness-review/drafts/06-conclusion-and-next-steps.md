# Conclusion and Next Steps

## Assessment outcome

WP2's architecture is sound and delivered (D2.1, D2.2). Execution is ahead of what a first read of the
pull requests alone would suggest: Use Case 1 is built, parameterised, and published; Use Case 2 is
built and awaiting real-event validation; Use Case 3 is well-specified and, while unbuilt, will be
validated near-term through a Charter-user (UNOSAT/ESA) review of Montandon rather than an
implementation, with ample runway to the December D2.4 deadline for the fuller build. Early results from
the National Society interviews (6 of 11 conducted) strongly validate Use Cases 1 and 2. The main items
standing between today and a fully demonstrated set of case studies are a handful of decisions and
finishing one already in-flight piece of integration work (CEMS and Charter reaching staging, hoped for
11 and around 17 September 2026 respectively), not open design questions.

## Topics for the Readiness Review meeting

- The baseline-computation strategy decision for Use Case 1 (Section 4.1, Section 5 item 1). This
  should be resolved at or before the meeting, since it is blocking and cheap to decide.
- Confirming CEMS's staging deployment (Section 4.2, Section 5 item 2), hoped for 11 September 2026,
  before the meeting, and Charter's shortly after, since this is the fastest path to a real-event
  validation of Use Case 2.
- Confirming Use Case 3's scope decision (Section 4.1, Section 5 item 6): a Charter-user (UNOSAT/ESA)
  review of Montandon rather than a build, ahead of the fuller implementation targeted for WP2 execution.
- Findings from the National Society interviews to date (Section 4.5): 6 of 11 conducted, strongly
  validating Use Cases 1 and 2, to ground the discussion in operational user feedback alongside the
  internal assessment.
- Status and timeline of the database remediation (Section 4.3), since it is the one risk that could
  affect all three use cases regardless of individual progress.

## Path to D2.4

This report is a checkpoint, not the finish line, and D2.4 is not due until December 2026. Use Case 1 is
close to demonstration-ready today. Use Case 2 needs a live event to validate against, which is close at
hand: CEMS is hoped for staging around 11 September 2026, with Charter about a week behind. Use Case 3's
strong specification gives it a clear starting point for its fuller build once its dependencies are
stable; in the meantime, the Charter-user (UNOSAT/ESA) review provides near-term validation of the
approach. The plan in Section 5 is scoped to keep that trajectory on track without forcing any use case
ahead of where its dependencies allow.
