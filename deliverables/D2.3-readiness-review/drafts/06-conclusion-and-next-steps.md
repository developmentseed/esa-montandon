# Conclusion and Next Steps

## Assessment outcome

WP2's architecture is sound and delivered (D2.1, D2.2); WP2's execution is real but not yet finished,
and the three use cases are at meaningfully different stages of that execution. Use Case 1 is close to
demonstrable pending one strategic decision and one data gap. Use Case 2 exists but is unvalidated.
Use Case 3 is well-specified but unbuilt, and honestly depends on the other two landing first. None of
this reflects an architectural problem — it reflects the normal shape of execution progress partway
through a work package, and we would rather report it plainly than round it up.

## What we recommend discussing at the Readiness Review meeting

- The baseline-computation strategy decision for Use Case 1 (Section 4.1, Section 5 item 1) — this
  should be resolved *at or before* the meeting, since it is blocking, cheap to decide, and unblocks
  downstream UX work.
- The scope of Use Case 3 for this milestone (Section 4.1, Section 5 item 6) — whether a partial build,
  a full build, or a specified-but-deferred status is the right target, given its dependency chain.
- Early signal from the National Society interviews (Section 4.4), to ground the discussion in
  operational user feedback rather than internal assessment alone.
- Status and timeline of the database remediation (Section 4.2), since it is the one risk that could
  affect all three use cases regardless of individual progress.

## Path to D2.4

This report is a checkpoint, not the finish line. Deliverable D2.4 will document the three use cases as
completed case studies, demonstrated against real events, with guidance for reuse in other application
contexts. The plan in Section 5 is designed to get each use case to that state on a realistic timeline —
prioritising a genuinely solid Use Case 1 and a validated Use Case 2 over a rushed, unvalidated Use Case
3, while keeping Use Case 3's strong specification work ready to build on as soon as its dependencies are
stable.
